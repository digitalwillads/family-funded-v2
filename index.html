What is the catch?

You put a card on file when we sign, not when you apply. Nothing is charged unless the first month hits the sales target we agree on together. If it does, that month's fee is charged and you stay on for three months. That is the whole trade.

What does "works" mean?

A sales number we agree on before launch, tracked in the ad account and checked against your Shopify orders. Not clicks, not traffic. Sales from people who were not already searching for your brand.

Why would you do this?

Because a family brand that grows stays with us for years. We would rather earn the first month than charge for it.

Who is this for?

Family focused ecommerce stores already making sales, on Shopify or similar, with a product that ships. If a mother is the one deciding, we know that account.

Who is it not for?

Brands still finding their first customers, and stores where one sale is worth too little for the first month to prove anything either way. We will tell you that on the call rather than take the bet.

Do you only work with baby and kids brands?

No. Family focused means the buyer is buying for a household. Diaper bags, planners, nursery furniture, sleep programs, outdoor gear. If a mother is the one deciding, we know that account.
